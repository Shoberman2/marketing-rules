# Hook Bank (202 Formulas, Tagged by Lever and Format)

Every formula includes a template and a generic example across four archetypes (`SaaS`, `News`, `Agency`, `Civic Data`). Body structure after the hook is covered in [`retention.md`](retention.md).

## Rules for using this bank

1. **Facts only.** Fill bracketed items like `[Bill #]`, `[$X]` or `[N]` from a primary source at post time. Never invent a number, quote or customer story.
2. **Pick a formula for its mechanism, not its exact words.** Write the hook fresh in your brand voice. Reused templates start reading like templates.
3. **Generate many and keep one.** Write 5 to 10 candidates. Score each 0 to 2 on Gap, Specificity, Truth, Fit, Voice and Pull (Truth and Pull are pass/fail gates). For short video, record two hooks: one as a question and one as a statement.
4. **The package must match the payoff.** The first beat after the hook confirms the promise. Winning the tap without delivering kills retention and trust.
5. **No em dashes.** Keep sentences crisp, specific and plain-English.
6. **Run [`checklist.md`](checklist.md) before publishing.**

**Lever tags:** `GAP` = Calibrated curiosity gap · `FLIP` = Prediction break / contrarian · `NUM` = Specificity / hard number · `SCENE` = Scene / story / in medias res · `CALL` = Identity callout · `STAKES` = Stakes / loss / cost · `PROOF` = Receipt / authority / proof-first · `USE` = Practical value · `TIME` = Timeliness / novelty · `VS` = Comparison / choice. Strong hooks often stack two levers.

## Format craft notes

- **X single post:** First 7 to 12 words carry the hook. Line 2 is the payoff of line 1, not setup. Native media beats a bare link; put links in a reply when testing reach.
- **X thread post 1:** State the claim, exact item count and order (ranked or escalating). Number posts. Put a re-hook at post 3 or 4. Close on the payoff plus one real question or link, with no "follow for more."
- **Reel / TikTok / Short:** Write three tracks separately: visual action (0 to 3 s), spoken line (<=2 s), and on-screen text (<=6 words) for sound-off viewers. Open on motion or the receipt, never a greeting. Loop the last line back into the first.
- **Carousel slide 1:** Must stand alone. Slide 2 starts delivering immediately with no intro slide. The count on slide 1 must match the slides delivered.
- **LinkedIn:** Roughly ~210 characters on desktop and ~140 on mobile show before "see more." Line 1 must stand alone; line 2 ends on an open beat.
- **Headline:** Name the subject and stakes, and hold back one mechanism. Use accurate negative words only where true; avoid blind questions or outrage on news.
- **YouTube title + thumbnail:** Thumbnail shows the object; title adds the stake without repeating the same words. Never put the answer inside the package.

## Anti-patterns (delete on sight)

1. **"Not X. Y." / "It's not just X, it's Y."** Top AI tell. State Y directly with proof.
2. **Throat-clearing and greetings:** "In today's post...", "Hey everyone", "Let's dive in", "Buckle up", "Hot take:".
3. **"Have you ever wondered...?" / "Picture this:" / "Imagine a world where..."**
4. **Rhetorical question + self-answer** ("The catch? There isn't one.").
5. **Colon reveals** ("The secret: consistency.").
6. **Unattributed percentages** ("90% of companies..."). Cite source and date or cut.
7. **Reflexive tricolons** ("Fast, secure, and scalable").
8. **Blind clickbait questions and outrage on news.** Lowers trust and engagement.
9. **Describing the visual** ("Here's a photo of...").
10. **Vague teases** ("You won't believe this").
11. **Giving away the payoff in the hook or thumbnail.**
12. **Overly broad address** ("Attention everyone").
13. **Overpromising:** Hook wins the tap and body fails to deliver.
14. **Em dashes.** Zero in posts, headlines or hooks.
