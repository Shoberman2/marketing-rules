# Spencer's Marketing Rules

The house playbook for hooks, human attention, retention and ethical social distribution. Any AI agent (Claude, Codex, Grok Bot) writing, reviewing or testing social posts, short videos, carousels, threads or headlines follows these rules.

| File | Covers |
| --- | --- |

| [`checklist.md`](checklist.md) | **Run before every post.** The 12-item pre-post check (9 attention items + 3 hard guardrails). |
| [`hooks.md`](hooks.md) | Full 202-formula hook bank tagged by 10 levers and 7 formats, with generic examples across SaaS, news, agency and civic data. |

| [`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) | Attributions and MIT license notices for open-source repos referenced here. |

## How to use

1. **Before writing:** Read [`MARKETING-RULES.md`](MARKETING-RULES.md) for platform ranking weights, audience awareness matching and retention rules.
2. **Drafting hooks:** Pick a mechanism (not a cliché) from [`hooks.md`](hooks.md), write 5 to 10 candidates, and keep the sharpest true one.
3. **Before publishing or sending a draft:** Score every piece against [`checklist.md`](checklist.md). A post must pass 12/12 on Hard Guardrails (items 10 to 12) and at least 7/9 on Attention & Craft (items 1 to 9).
4. **Every week:** Keep a 5 to 10 post swipe file, run one single-variable hook experiment, and drop formats that lose to your baseline median.
