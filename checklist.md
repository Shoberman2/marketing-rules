# Pre-Post Human Attention Checklist (12 Items)

Run every original post, thread opener, short-video script, carousel cover, LinkedIn post and headline through this 12-item check before publishing or sending a draft for review.

**Pass rule:** A post must score **100% on Hard Guardrails (items 10 to 12)** and at least **7/9 on Attention & Craft (items 1 to 9)**. If it fails, rewrite the opening line or cut the post.

## Attention & Craft (Items 1 to 9)

1. **First-moment stop:** Does line 1 (or seconds 0 to 3 of video / Slide 1) create a calibrated gap, prediction break or concrete scene without background throat-clearing? Cut your first draft sentence and test if sentence two is sharper. Write at least 5 candidate hooks and keep the best one; for video, record both a question version and a statement version.
2. **Moderate concreteness (neither blind clickbait nor spoiler):** Does the opener name the real subject and stakes while holding back the *how* or *twist* for line 2?
3. **Rule of One:** Is there exactly one reader, one core idea and one payoff? Remove competing secondary points or extra CTAs.
4. **Artifact or hard number:** Does the post carry at least one verifiable noun, dollar figure, document/docket number, verbatim quote or screenshot? Zero vague "many companies" or "90% of people" claims.
5. **Group-Chat / Save Test:** Would one specific person in your target audience actually bookmark this to use this week or DM it to a friend?
6. **Awareness match:** Does the opener match where the reader actually is (usually Unaware or Problem-Aware in a feed, rather than pitching product features cold)?
7. **Two-lever video check (Reels / TikTok / Shorts only):** Are the spoken hook (<=2 seconds) and on-screen text (<=6 words, <=1 second) written separately, with visual motion in frame 0:00 and the payoff delivered before the halfway mark?
8. **AI-tell & slop purge:** Zero banned LLM tells ("Not X. Y.", "In today's fast-paced world", "Here's the kicker:", "Let's dive in", rhetorical question + self-answer, colon reveals, reflexive threes, em dashes)?
9. **Body holds, ending is abrupt:** Does the first beat after the hook confirm its promise? If the piece is longer than one screen (thread, carousel, short video, article), is there a re-hook around the one-third to one-half mark? Does it stop the instant the payoff lands, with no recap and no "follow me for more"?

## Hard Guardrails (Items 10 to 12, Must All Pass)

10. **100% payoff & honest framing:** Does the body completely deliver what the hook promised, with zero deceptive withholding, fake deadlines or hollow question-headline clickbait?
11. **Verified facts & primary sources:** Is every product stat, price, customer story or dated count verified against real records, and does every news or data claim cite a primary source?
12. **Right account, right autonomy:** Does this post match the brand's voice, autonomy level (draft-only vs. auto-publish) and channel boundaries (no off-brand politics on product accounts, no unapproved claims or confidential project leaks)?
